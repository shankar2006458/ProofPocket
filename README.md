# ProofPocket 📦

**ProofPocket** is a simple, offline-first web app that helps you manage your products, receipts, and warranty information in one place.

## ✨ Features

- 📋 **Product Management** – Add, edit, and delete products.
- 🧾 **Receipt Storage** – Attach receipts to your products.
- 🛡️ **Warranty Tracking** – Track warranty expiration dates.
- 🔔 **Warranty Notifications** – Get browser notifications for upcoming expirations.
- 🔍 **Search and Filters** – Quickly find products.
- 📊 **Dashboard** – View product statistics at a glance.
- 📅 **Calendar Export** – Export warranty expiration dates to your calendar.
- 💾 **Local Storage** – Store your data directly in your browser.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Works on desktop and mobile devices.
